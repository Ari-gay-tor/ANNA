# ANNA: start here

When you don't know what to do, ask ANNA.
She is a helper for the moments you feel stuck, overwhelmed or unsure. She runs on your own PC.

## 1. Install (about 5 minutes)

You need internet for this, once.

1. Unzip the folder anywhere, for example in Documents (right-click the zip, then "Extract All").
2. Open the folder and double-click `install-anna.cmd`.
3. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. If it says Node.js is needed: type Y, press Enter, and approve the Windows question.
5. Wait. It counts Step 1/5 to Step 5/5. When it says SUCCESS, ANNA opens by herself.

## 2. Get your free key

ANNA needs a free Gemini key to think. She asks for it the first time she opens.

1. Go to aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with a Google account.
2. Use a Google account or project with no billing set up. Then it can never cost you money.
3. Click "Create API key" and copy the key.
4. Paste it into ANNA's setup screen and click "Check and save".

The key stays on this PC. You can change it later: click the gear icon at the top, then Settings.

## 3. Using ANNA

- Open ANNA from the ANNA icon on your Desktop whenever you are stuck, overwhelmed or unsure.
- Say what is going on, in your own words. Short is fine.
- She can remember things and set reminders when you ask. For example: "Remember that I work best in the morning." or "Remind me at 6 PM to call Dad."
- See everything she knows about you on the Memory page. You can edit or delete anything there.

## 4. When ANNA gets it wrong

1. Click the small flag ("Not helpful") under her reply. It shows when you hover over the reply.
2. Write one line about what you needed instead, then click Save.
3. At the end of the week, open the Feedback page and press "Export for Ari".
4. Send Ari the file it saves (anna-feedback-DATE.md).

The file has only the replies you flagged and the messages just before them. Read it before you send it.

## 5. Privacy

- Everything is stored only on your PC.
- Your messages are sent to Google's Gemini API to get replies. On Gemini's free tier, Google may use what you send to improve its products. So don't share anything you wouldn't want a stranger to read.
- Ari only sees what you choose to export.

## 6. Update, uninstall, help

Update:

1. Unzip the new version, over the old folder or anywhere else.
2. Double-click `install-anna.cmd` again.
3. Your data is kept. It lives outside the folder, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ANNA.

Uninstall:

1. Double-click `uninstall-anna.cmd`.
2. Your data stays in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ANNA. To remove everything, delete that folder. (Press the Windows key + R, type %LOCALAPPDATA%\ANNA and press Enter to open it.)

Help: if something goes wrong, send Ari the file %LOCALAPPDATA%\ANNA\logs\anna.log. It does not contain your key.
